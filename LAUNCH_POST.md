Most of us don't find out about an HPOP leak or a dying CPS until the truck won't start. By then it's a tow bill plus whatever the shop finds.

I built Diesel Sentry to catch that stuff early. It's an Android app that connects to an OBDLink MX+ over Bluetooth, auto-detects your engine from the VIN (7.3, 6.0, 6.4, or 6.7), and watches 24 live parameters every drive.
